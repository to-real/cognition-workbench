# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful place for careful disagreement and collaborative learning.

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- assume good intent while remaining precise about impact;
- welcome different levels of experience and different viewpoints;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of private information;
- accept maintainer decisions about scope and moderation.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe, constructive community. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed in good faith and kept confidential where possible.
